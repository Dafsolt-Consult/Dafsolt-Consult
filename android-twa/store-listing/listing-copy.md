## Full description (max 4000 characters)

Dafsolt BOS for School is the complete operating system for your school —
Academics, Finance, Computer-Based Testing (CBT), HR, Communication,
Operations and Analytics, all connected in one place.

Built for African schools, from single campuses to multi-campus groups.

WHAT'S INSIDE

Academics
- Student records, class & subject management, timetabling
- Attendance tracking for staff and students
- Results entry and report cards
- Lesson plans and e-learning content

Computer-Based Testing (CBT)
- Question bank shared across exams
- Timed exams with auto-grading for objective questions
- A dedicated exam-kiosk mode for supervised exam halls
- Practice mode for exam preparation

Finance
- Fee schedules and payment tracking
- Per-term financial summaries

HR & Payroll
- Staff records, attendance, shift scheduling
- Leave/time-off requests
- Payroll figures (numbers only — no payment processing)

Communication
- Announcements and a school calendar
- Parent portal with real-time access to their child's records

Operations
- Library catalog and borrow-record tracking
- Transport and hostel management
- Health and disciplinary records
- Inventory and asset tracking

Analytics & Reporting
- Business overview dashboards for school administrators
- Compliance reports

WHO IT'S FOR
- School proprietors and administrators
- Teachers
- Bursars and accountants
- Parents and guardians
- Students

Every account is role-based — a teacher, a parent, and an administrator
each see only what's relevant to them.

Founding Schools Programme pricing is available for your school's first
year, across four self-serve plans plus a custom tier for multi-campus
school groups.

## Content rating guidance
General audience business/productivity app for school staff, students,
and parents. Not designed for children to install independently — school
devices (including CBT exam kiosks) are provisioned and managed by the
school, not personal downloads by minors.
